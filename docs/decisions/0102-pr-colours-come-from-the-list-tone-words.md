# A PR's colour comes from the list's tone words

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Area:** [Reading sessions](../architecture/sessions.md#reading-sessions)

## Decision

A PR's colour comes from the tone words a list panel's rows use, never a palette of its own.

## Turned down

- GitHub's own colours: they are a second palette, and their green for "open" says the opposite of the dot's green for "done" on the same row.
