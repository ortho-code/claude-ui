# A session's kebab is complete and a button is a shortcut

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Area:** [A session's menu holds everything about it](../architecture/claude.md#a-sessions-menu-holds-everything-about-it)

## Decision

The kebab is complete; a button is a shortcut: every button, control dot and mark that opens something on a session's surfaces has its item in the menu.

## Why

It was the other way round before: the tab's and the strip's kebab left out the button, "which stays in sight", and the list had no plain stop, only Pause, as there was then, and Archive, which stopped a running session on the way.

## Turned down

- Leaving the button out of the kebab because it stays in sight, as the tab's and the strip's kebab did.
