# A list row's href was dropped for open-url

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Area:** [Types from the config folder](../architecture/panels.md#types-from-the-config-folder)

## Decision

A row's or a part's `href` was dropped for the `open-url` verb.

## Why

Panel types had not been released, and two ways to say one thing is what one behaviour per thing warns against.

## Turned down

- Keeping `href` as a short form: two ways to say one thing is what one behaviour per thing warns against.
