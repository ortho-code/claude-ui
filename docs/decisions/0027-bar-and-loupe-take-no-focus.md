# The bar and the loupe take no focus

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Area:** [The bar and the loupe](../architecture/history.md#the-bar-and-the-loupe)

## Decision

Neither the bar nor the loupe takes the focus, so the terminal or the history keeps the keys.

## Turned down

- Leaving the focus where a click on either put it: it used to leave the focus nowhere, and Esc and the step keys with it.
