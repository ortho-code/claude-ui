# The tab bar folds from a chevron on a line under it

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Area:** [UI conventions](../architecture/ui-conventions.md#ui-conventions)

## Decision

The tab bar folds from the chevron a panel group folds from, laid on a line under the bar.

## Turned down

- A button at the right end of the bar's top row, as it first was: it is the variant the divider's chevron had already beaten in the layout's mock, a button in the panel's own bar; it also took a column of width from every row, and in All sat beside a row that can be a project's name alone, out of line with the tabs.
