# The folded tab bar's chevron turns into a hidden tab's dot

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Area:** [UI conventions](../architecture/ui-conventions.md#ui-conventions)

## Decision

While a tab the fold hides asks something of you, the chevron turns into that tab's status dot, in the arrow's place and at full strength.

## Turned down

- The dot on the chevron's corner, as a rail icon's does, for every status busy included, as it first sat: it took the chevron's resting fade, and a busy ring at that strength on the corner of an 11px tab read badly.
- A dot on the line beside the chevron: it would need a click of its own and could read as a second control.
- A dot per hidden tab at the end of the row: it could wrap the folded bar onto a second line, while the session list already says which session it is.
