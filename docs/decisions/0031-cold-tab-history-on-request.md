# A tab with no claude shows its history only on request

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Area:** [Opening it, and getting out](../architecture/history.md#opening-it-and-getting-out)

## Decision

A tab with no claude behind it shows the pane's sentence with Resume and Show history, and the bar comes with Show history.

## Turned down

- Showing the history there by itself, at launch: it was not clear what had happened or why it was shown.
- The bar on that sentence, as it was at first: until Show history there is nothing for it to scroll.
