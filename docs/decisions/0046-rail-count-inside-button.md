# A rail icon's count sits inside the button

- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Area:** [Types from the config folder](../architecture/panels.md#types-from-the-config-folder)

## Decision

The count on a rail icon sits inside the button, under the icon on a vertical rail and beside it on a horizontal one.

## Turned down

- A corner badge, tried first: at 3x even "9+" covered the whole icon on a 24px button, while the rail clips anything past its 28px.
