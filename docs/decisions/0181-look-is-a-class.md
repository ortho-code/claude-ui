# A look reaches a status dot as a class

- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Area:** [UI conventions](../architecture/ui-conventions.md#ui-conventions)

## Decision

Every status dot carries its look as a class, `look-<id>`, beside its state's.

## Why

The dots of a menu or a header travel as class strings.

## Turned down

- A `data-` attribute: the dots of a menu or a header travel as class strings.
