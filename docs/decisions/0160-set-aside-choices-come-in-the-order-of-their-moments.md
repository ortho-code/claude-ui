# Set-aside choices come in the order of their moments

- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Area:** [Status cues](../architecture/status-cues.md#status-cues)

## Decision

The set-aside choices are offered in the order their moments come, worked out when the dialog opens, then Pick a date…, then Whenever.

## Why

A day is what comes to hand first, since an undated mark brings nothing back by itself.

## Turned down

- An order of your own: the moments give one.
