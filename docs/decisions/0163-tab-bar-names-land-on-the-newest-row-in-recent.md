# The tab bar's names land on the newest row in Recent

- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Area:** [Recent](../architecture/session-list.md#recent)

## Decision

In Recent, a press on the tab bar's names lands on the newest row drawn of that project, or of that group, and flashes it.

## Why

There is no heading to land on in Recent.

## Turned down

- Switching the list to Grouped instead: a press on a tab-bar name would then change an arrangement chosen in the list.
