# Each tab keeps its place in the history

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Area:** [Opening it, and getting out](../architecture/history.md#opening-it-and-getting-out)

## Decision

Leaving a tab with its history open remembers where, and coming back reopens it there.

## Turned down

- Closing the history on every tab switch, as at first: it lost your place in it whenever you looked at another tab.
