# An on/off in Settings is a switch

- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Area:** [UI conventions](../architecture/ui-conventions.md#ui-conventions)

## Decision

In Settings, an on/off is a switch beside the words for what it turns on: the native checkbox drawn as one (`.settings-switch`) and carrying `role="switch"`.

## Why

The mocks drew it as a switch.

## Turned down

- The plain checkbox, which it replaced.
