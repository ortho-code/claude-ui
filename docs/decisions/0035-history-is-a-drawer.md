# The history is a drawer from the bar

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Area:** [Opening it, and getting out](../architecture/history.md#opening-it-and-getting-out)

## Decision

The history slides in from the right edge, beside the bar it was opened from, over about two-thirds of the pane, with claude in view on the left under a scrim, and one labelled control, "Back to live", to get out.

## Why

The first versions covered the whole pane, and "how do I get back?" was the question they left.

## Turned down

- Covering the whole pane, as the first versions did: "how do I get back?" was the question they left.
- The upper two-thirds, leaving the lower third to claude, where a click went back and reached claude as well: in use it read as a split panel rather than something laid over claude.
