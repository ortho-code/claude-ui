# Settings shows needs-you without an editing screen yet

- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Area:** [Status cues](../architecture/status-cues.md#status-cues)

## Decision

Settings lists the needs-you categories and types as they stand, has no switch for them yet, and names where they are set.

## Why

An editing screen waits until what people change in `needsYou` shows what it should hold.
