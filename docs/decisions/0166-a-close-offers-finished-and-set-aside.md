# A close's toast also offers Finished and Set aside

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Area:** [A session's menu holds everything about it](../architecture/terminal.md#a-sessions-menu-holds-everything-about-it)

## Decision

A close's toast also offers Finished and Set aside… (`offerUndoClose`).

## Why

The moment a session leaves your hands is when what becomes of it is cheapest to decide.

## Turned down

- A question at every close: it would be a dialog in the way.
