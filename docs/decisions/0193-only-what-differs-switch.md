# What differs from the defaults is a switch, not a section

- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Area:** [The config folder](../architecture/panels.md#the-config-folder)

## Decision

The overview of what differs from the defaults is a switch at the foot of the list of sections, Only what differs, which narrows every section to those settings.

## Why

What differs belongs at the setting, where it is changed.

## Turned down

- A section of its own, Changed from the defaults, seen in use: it repeated every setting a second time, away from where it is changed.
