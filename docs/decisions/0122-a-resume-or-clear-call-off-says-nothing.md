# A resume or clear that calls a finish off says nothing of it

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Area:** [A session's menu holds everything about it](../architecture/claude.md#a-sessions-menu-holds-everything-about-it)

## Decision

A `/resume` or `/clear` says nothing of the call-off of a finish, and one into a session another tab runs already says that it runs there.

## Why

You typed it.
