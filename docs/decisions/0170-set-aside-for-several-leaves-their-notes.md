# Setting several sessions aside leaves their notes as they are

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Area:** [Selecting several](../architecture/session-list.md#selecting-several)

## Decision

With several sessions ticked, the bar's Set aside… dialog has no line of where they stand and says their notes stay as they are.
With one ticked it is that session's dialog, its note included.

## Why

One text over many sessions would write over notes that each say something else.

## Turned down

- Taking Set aside… out of the bar, or offering it only with one ticked: Keep already holds a session out of the review without a day, the line is optional for one session too, and the review needs an act that sets aside to be emptied.
