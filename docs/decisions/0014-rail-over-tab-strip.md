# A group switches panels from a rail of icons

- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Area:** [Several panels in a group](../architecture/panels.md#several-panels-in-a-group)

## Decision

A group with several panels switches from a rail of icons on the edge it folds toward.

## Why

With the rail, the terminal area keeps its own tab bar and gets no header.

## Turned down

- A tab strip, tried beside the rail: a strip costs a row and changes shape when its group folds, and a built-in sharing a group with a strip put two rows of tabs above the terminal.
